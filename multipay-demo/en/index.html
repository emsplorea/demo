Done Multipay

Serve three guests at once – without losing track.

With Multipay the till sends each order to a free card terminal, and the bartender takes the next guest while cards are processed. Up to six orders can be open at once.

The till always shows which terminal is free, busy or waiting for an answer – and a payment with an unknown outcome is never sent again before it has been checked.

How it works

Four things to know about Multipay

Multipay only changes what happens after the order is rung up. The rest of the till works as before.

  • Up to six orders
  • One button per terminal
  • Status per payment
  1. 1Order and send
  2. 2Several guests
  3. 3Exceptions
  4. 4Setup

  1. 01Chapter 1 of 4

    Order and send with one tap

    Ring up the order and tap a free terminal. The order goes there, and the till is ready for the next guest. The sent order shows “Waiting for payment” with the terminal and a timer.

    • One button per card terminal
    • Free, busy or not connected shows on the button
    • A payment can be cancelled without touching the others

  2. 02Chapter 2 of 4

    Serve several guests at once

    Each guest gets their own order tab – up to six. While one pays, you ring up the next. An approved payment closes its order by itself and frees the terminal, without moving you away from the order you are working on.

    • Order tabs with amount and terminal
    • Auto Switch opens a new order once you have sent one
    • Fold the totals panel for more room for order lines

  3. 03Chapter 3 of 4

    Exceptions are handled safely

    If a card is declined, the order gets “Try again”, and you can send it to any free terminal. If the terminal does not answer, the payment shows as unknown: it cannot be sent again until “Check status” has fetched the answer. “Release” only frees the terminal – the payment stays unresolved until it is checked.

    • Declined: try again, also on another terminal
    • Unknown: only “Check status” – never a new payment on the same order
    • Payments on the other terminals carry on

  4. 04Chapter 4 of 4

    Setup and administration

    Multipay is a per-till setting in admin, together with the Auto Switch default. The till needs at least one card terminal and is used without a table map. It is made for PC and tablet.

    • With “card only”: one button per terminal, as in the demo
    • Without “card only”: the normal payment screen is used, cash included, and orders can still stay open side by side
    • Auto Switch can be overridden in the till
