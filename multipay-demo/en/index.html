Done Multipay

One till, several terminals – the bar that never stops.

With Multipay the till sends each order to a free card terminal and is ready for the next guest straight away. Guests pay on the terminal while the bartender rings up the next order.

Up to six orders can be open at once, and the till always shows which terminal is free, busy or waiting for an answer.

How it works

The till does the work – the terminals take payment

Multipay only changes what happens after the order is rung up. The rest of the till works as before.

  • Up to six orders
  • One button per terminal
  • Status per payment

  1. 01Step 1 of 10

    Several orders at once

    Each guest gets their own order tab at the top of the till – up to six at once. The tab shows the amount and which terminal the order was sent to.

    • Switch between orders with one tap
    • A new order with +
    • Sent orders cannot be changed

  2. 02Step 2 of 10

    One button per terminal

    Under the basket there is one button per card terminal. The till shows which ones are free, and one tap sends the order there.

    • Free, busy or not connected
    • The number of free terminals is shown
    • Names from the terminal setup

  3. 03Step 3 of 10

    Each payment is tracked

    A sent order shows “Waiting for payment” with the amount, the terminal and a timer. The payment can be cancelled without touching the others.

    • A timer per payment
    • Cancel just this payment
    • Ring up other orders meanwhile

  4. 04Step 4 of 10

    Paid – and the terminal is free

    When the card is approved, the order closes by itself, and the terminal is free for the next guest.

    • No extra taps after payment
    • The terminal is freed at once

  5. 05Step 5 of 10

    A declined card

    If the card is declined, the order shows “Payment declined” with “Try again” and “Cancel order”. Payments on the other terminals carry on.

    • Try again on the same order
    • The tab shows declined in red

  6. 06Step 6 of 10

    Unknown outcome – check before sending again

    If the till gets no answer from the terminal, the payment shows as “Unknown”. The terminal is held for a short while, so nobody sends a new payment before it has been checked.

    • A warning above the send buttons
    • A countdown on the terminal
    • One payment at a time per terminal

  7. 07Step 7 of 10

    Release the terminal once it is checked

    Once the staff member has looked at the terminal and it is empty, it is released with “Release”. The till asks for confirmation first.

    • Confirmation before release
    • The terminal is ready for new payments

  8. 08Step 8 of 10

    Auto Switch

    With “Auto Switch” the till opens a new order as soon as an order is sent. The default is set per till and can be overridden in the till.

    • Straight on to the next guest
    • On or off in the till’s top bar

  9. 09Step 9 of 10

    More room for the order lines

    The panel with VAT, order type, tip and discount under the total can be folded. Then only the total shows, and there is room for more order lines.

    • One tap on “Total”
    • The total always shows

  10. 10Step 10 of 10

    Made for PC and tablet

    Multipay is made for tills on a PC or tablet at the bar or kiosk. On a tablet the send buttons sit in two columns.

    • PC and tablet
    • The same menu and settlement as the rest of the till

Setup

Turned on per till

Multipay is a device setting in admin. The till needs at least one card terminal.

  • One setting

    Multipay is turned on for the till in admin, together with the Auto Switch default.

  • One or more terminals

    The till uses the card terminals set up on the device.

  • Without a table map

    Multipay is used on tills without a table map. Card only is recommended.
